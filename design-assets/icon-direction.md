# 便签图标的系列区分

主识别是暖珊瑚色底、大张奶白色折角便签纸与三条粗书写横线。纸张占主体，Itsuki 仅作为右下角印记；人物特征约占图标面积的 10% 至 15%，不再采用大幅紫色人物头像。菜单栏以折角纸张的单色轮廓作为主识别，小星形留白只作系列签名。

系列原则：不同软件各自使用占据主要面积的业务物体、不同的色域与不同的轮廓；统一圆角比例、材质与小尺寸角色印记。不能只在相同人物头像旁边替换一个小配件。

使用内置 Image Gen，应用图标参考旧版 Itsuki 图标中的人物身份，菜单栏图标为独立生成。原始人物设定和头像来自 ~/project_itsuki。

应用图标最终提示词：

Redesign this macOS sticky note app icon with a radically different primary hierarchy so it cannot be confused with another purple character portrait database app. Character reference identity only. ONE finished square app icon asset on transparent canvas, no mockup or text. Centered rounded-square satin warm amber/coral background occupying 80% of canvas with consistent macOS corner geometry and 10% transparent margins on all sides. The dominant motif is a huge pale ivory sticky-note sheet, slightly tilted counterclockwise 5 degrees, occupying about 75% of tile area, large unmistakable folded upper right corner and a subtly curling lower corner. Three broad dark dusty terracotta handwritten-like horizontal strokes in upper half of sheet, clearly read as writing at 32px but NOT actual letters. In bottom-right corner of sheet a SMALL flat muted-plum Itsuki head silhouette cameo, only 15% of total tile area: high ponytail, bow, simple profile facing left, no eyes, no thin hair strands. Cameo feels like a simple small printed stamp on the paper, not a portrait or separate badge. NO large character hair anywhere. Object-first icon: warm color and giant cream folded paper should dominate even when squinting. Character is a secondary family signature. Refined lightly tactile macOS app icon, clean strong contrast, flat shapes, restrained tiny shadows only, no glossy purple frame, no rim glow, no database cylinder, no ornaments beyond the character stamp. Entire tile with all paper fully inside safe area, never protrudes. Professional polished clear minimal geometry, canvas transparent outside rounded square.

菜单栏最终提示词：

Create a technical monochrome macOS menu bar template icon for a sticky note app. One simple solid black folded sheet of paper icon on transparent background. Square canvas, central 80% occupied by an upright paper sheet silhouette with a large dog-eared upper-right corner visibly delineated by a broad transparent diagonal gap. Two short thick transparent horizontal writing strokes in upper-left part of the black paper. Very small four-point star transparent cutout near lower right as character-family signature. No character head or hair, no rounded square background tile, no color, no gray, no white pixels, no shading, no shadow, no lettering. At 18px the BLACK PAPER outline and folded corner must be the unmistakable primary form, simple stout shapes, clear large negative space. Single finished scalable vector-like raster symbol. Clean transparent outside and transparent negative spaces.
